Hyperscaler Radar

Eleven open-source CLIs, one per platform, that scan an account and turn it into an inventory, a cost report, and a draw.io diagram — built and released solo under the morsmi PyPI namespace.

aws-radar — Aug 28, 2026

The suite began as a single aws-radar package and grew iteratively — one platform, one billing quirk, one customer conversation at a time. Today it covers AWS, GCP, Azure, and OCI inventory scanning, plus Datadog, OpenAI, Amazon Bedrock, and CoreWeave, each shipped as its own focused CLI rather than one do-everything tool.

The core pattern repeats across clouds: scan the account, export a CSV, generate a draw.io architecture diagram, and surface cost-waste findings — the same inventory and diagram pair that the sa-review-report skill consumes for customer SA reviews at Pump.

The suite

  • aws-radar Last released Aug 28, 2026

    AWS resource inventory scanner, Cost Explorer reporter, and draw.io architecture diagram generator.

  • openai-radar Last released Aug 24, 2026

    OpenAI infrastructure FinOps SDK — scan, analyze, and export your OpenAI org resources.

  • azure-radar Last released Aug 19, 2026

    CLI tool for Azure services inventory and diagramming.

  • datadog-radar Last released Jul 29, 2026

    Observability FinOps scanner for Datadog — cost, usage, custom metric cardinality, and waste findings from one CLI command.

  • gcp-billing-radar Last released Jul 10, 2026

    Scans GCP billing accounts and bulk re-links their projects to a new account atomically, with dry-run, verification, CSV results, and an auto-generated rollback plan.

  • oci-radar Last released Jul 10, 2026

    CLI tool for OCI services inventory and diagramming.

  • gcp-radar Last released Jun 24, 2026

    GCP resource inventory scanner and draw.io architecture diagram generator.

  • aws-bedrock-radar Last released Jun 22, 2026

    Serverless radar for Amazon Bedrock — deploys a Lambda that logs prompt and latency data to S3, then analyzes it.

  • aws-waste-radar Last released Jun 11, 2026

    Scans AWS accounts for storage and EC2 cost-waste findings, including snapshot cleanup candidates.
